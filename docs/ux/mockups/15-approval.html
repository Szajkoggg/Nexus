SC1

Keep or Discard in the server, the briefing and the notices

The server works out what a stopped step left: its own commits from git.yaml, without those marked foreign, and the uncommitted changes of the queue's checkout, labelled as not necessarily the step's. Continue and the approval of a new revision of a stopped queue take a choice, Keep or Discard, required when the step left commits. Keep records it and lifts as today; Discard records it, does not lift, and tells the orchestrator.
